Plot a straight line from your device to your own PC.

Jochona is a game-streaming client for Sunshine, Apollo, and Vibepollo hosts you run yourself: no account, no cloud relay, just the plotted course to your own network.

The course

three legs, one connection

Every stream follows the same short course: your device, your network, your host. Nothing routes through a server in between.

Your device

· any OS

Windows, macOS, Linux, or a Steam Deck docked to the TV. Controller-first navigation means every ordinary workflow works without a keyboard or mouse.

Your network

· LAN or Tailscale

Stream over your LAN, or reach a host through a private overlay like Tailscale. Wake-on-LAN starts a sleeping host, even when its cached MAC address has gone stale.

Your host

· capability-aware

Sunshine, Apollo, or Vibepollo. Jochona reads what each host actually supports, from Apollo's probe fields to Vibepollo's declarative capability endpoint, and shows only the features that host has.

Nothing between you and your host.

local by construction

Jochona talks straight to hosts you run. There's nothing in between to trust: every host you pair with is remembered on your device, and nothing syncs to a server, because there is no server.

Secrets never touch a database or a plain-text config file. They live in your OS's own credential vault:

  • macOSKeychain
  • WindowsDPAPI
  • Linuxlibsecret
HARD STOP

If a paired host's identity changes (a reinstalled Sunshine, a swapped machine behind the same address), Jochona halts the connection until you deliberately re-pair, with no silent continue.

Principles

what governs the build
  • »

    Excellent defaults, visible control.

    Sensible settings out of the box, every one of them reachable and changeable.

  • »

    Controller-first, not controller-only.

    Every ordinary workflow works with a gamepad from the couch. Keyboard and mouse still work too.

  • »

    Direct by default.

    The interface is driven by what your host can actually do, never by guessing a version string.

  • »

    Recover rather than fail.

    Diagnostics point at the likely cause instead of leaving you with a dead connection.

  • »

    Local ownership.

    No account, no cloud dependency. Secrets stay in your OS's own vault.
