Your games are already on your PC.

Jochona gets you to them from the couch, the desk, or a Steam Deck. The stream runs over your own network, and no Jochona service sits in the middle. It's a streaming client for the Sunshine, Apollo, and Vibepollo hosts you already run.

The whole route is yours.

There are only three pieces: the screen in your hands, the network in your house, and the PC doing the heavy lifting. Jochona keeps them talking directly and stays out of the way.

DEVICE → NETWORK → HOST

Your device

Windows, macOS, Linux, a Steam Deck, or a Windows handheld like the ROG Ally. Everything works from a controller on the couch; a keyboard is welcome, never required.

DESK · COUCH · HANDHELD

Your network

Stay on your LAN, or reach home through a private overlay like Tailscale. On the LAN the path is direct; over the overlay it stays end-to-end encrypted, and it never touches a Jochona server.

LAN · PRIVATE OVERLAY

Your host

Sunshine, Apollo, or Vibepollo. Jochona asks the host what it can actually do and shows you those controls, instead of guessing from a version number.

CAPABILITY-AWARE

There's nothing in the middle.

There's no Jochona account, no Jochona relay, and no Jochona server your stream could detour through. We didn't build any of them, on purpose.

Your pairings stay on your device, and credentials go in the vault your operating system already guards. Never a config file, never plain text.

  • Good defaults, visible controls.

    Sensible settings arrive ready, and every one of them stays reachable when you want something different.

  • Built to recover, not just fail.

    When something breaks, diagnostics name the likely cause and point at the next useful step, not just an error code.

HOST IDENTITY CHANGED

If your host changes, Jochona stops and asks.

A reinstalled host, or a different machine answering at the same address, is blocked until you deliberately re-pair. Nothing gets trusted quietly.

Where this is headed.

By 1.0

One library across every host you pair, per-game streaming profiles, and certified end-to-end paths for 4K 120 Hz HDR with AV1 where the whole hardware chain supports it.

Planned for later

A self-hosted companion service for remote wake, trusted-friend access, and an optional relay when a direct path fails. Everything stays open source and self-hostable, and a Jochona account never becomes mandatory.

Help shape it before 1.0.

Jochona is being built in the open and hasn't tagged a release yet. Watch the repository, read the code, or grab a fresh CI build and tell us what breaks.
